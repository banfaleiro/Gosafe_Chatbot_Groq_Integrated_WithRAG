GOSAFE SERVICES

1. FIRE EXTINGUISHER SALES, REFILLING AND SERVICING
Gosafe supplies all classes of fire extinguishers - ABC dry powder, CO2, water CO2, foam (AFFF/mechanical foam), clean agent (Class C), and wet chemical extinguishers for kitchen fires (Class F/K). Gosafe operates its own in-house refilling and hydro-testing workshop, so extinguishers are refilled, pressure-tested, and certified without third-party delays. Annual refilling, statutory hydro-testing every 5 years (for CO2 and certain cylinders), and on-site inspection tagging are all handled by Gosafe.

2. FIRE HOSE REEL SYSTEMS
Supply, installation, testing, and maintenance of fire hose reel systems and landing valves for commercial buildings, factories, and high-rise structures, in line with fire code requirements.

3. FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEMS
Design, supply, and installation of automatic fire suppression systems including:
- Clean agent suppression systems (for server rooms, electrical panels, data centers)
- CO2 flooding systems
- Kitchen fire suppression systems (wet chemical, for commercial kitchens and restaurants)
- Water mist systems

4. FIRE ALARM SYSTEMS AND FIRE PANELS
Design, supply, and installation of conventional and addressable fire alarm panels, smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual call points, hooters, and strobes. Gosafe also undertakes servicing, fault diagnosis, and upgrades of existing fire panels from various manufacturers.

5. SPRINKLER SYSTEMS
Design, supply, installation, and maintenance of automatic wet-pipe and dry-pipe sprinkler systems for warehouses, factories, hotels, and commercial complexes, including sprinkler heads, control valves, and fire pump integration.

6. FIRE HYDRANT SYSTEMS
Supply and installation of external and internal fire hydrant systems, including hydrant valves, fire pumps (electric, diesel, and jockey pumps), fire pump rooms, underground and overhead fire water storage tanks, and hydrant piping networks.

7. CCTV AND SURVEILLANCE SYSTEMS
Supply and installation of CCTV cameras (analog and IP-based), DVR/NVR systems, and remote monitoring solutions for homes, offices, and industrial sites.

8. BURGLAR ALARM SYSTEMS
Installation of intrusion detection systems including door/window contact sensors, motion detectors, and control panels linked to alarms or monitoring stations.

9. ACCESS CONTROL SYSTEMS
Supply and installation of access control solutions including biometric systems, RFID card access, and electronic door locks for offices and restricted areas.

10. GENERAL SAFETY EQUIPMENT
Supply of safety signage, emergency lighting, fire blankets, first aid kits, personal protective equipment (PPE), safety helmets, and other general workplace safety equipment.

11. ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACTS (AMC)
Gosafe specializes in AMC packages for commercial and industrial properties - hotels, factories, hospitals, offices, and residential societies - covering periodic inspection, testing, servicing, and statutory compliance documentation for all installed fire and safety equipment, so clients never have to worry about missed inspection dates or non-compliance during fire audits.

12. FIRE SAFETY AUDITS AND CONSULTANCY
Site surveys, fire risk assessments, and consultancy on fire safety compliance as per local fire department and NBC (National Building Code) norms, including support with fire NOC (No Objection Certificate) documentation.

13. TRAINING
Fire safety awareness training and mock fire drills for staff at commercial and industrial establishments, including hands-on extinguisher use training.
