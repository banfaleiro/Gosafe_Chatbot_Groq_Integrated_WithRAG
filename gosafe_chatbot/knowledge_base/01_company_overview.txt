GOSAFE FIRE, SECURITY AND SAFETY - COMPANY OVERVIEW

Gosafe Fire, Security and Safety is a leading fire and safety equipment company based in Goa, India.

Address: Shop No. 5, Stadium Road, Near KTC, Fatorda, Madgaon, Goa 403601.

Established in 2011, Gosafe has grown to become one of the leading companies dealing in fire equipment in Goa. With over a decade of experience, the company has built a strong reputation for reliability, quality workmanship, and prompt service across residential, commercial, and industrial establishments in Goa.

Gosafe is engaged in the design, supply, installation, and maintenance of:
- Fire suppression systems
- Fire alarm systems
- Fire hydrants and hose reel systems
- Fire extinguishers
- Sprinkler systems
- CCTV systems
- Burglar alarms
- Access control systems
- General safety equipment

Gosafe also operates a fully equipped fire extinguisher refilling workshop, allowing the company to service, refill, and hydro-test extinguishers in-house rather than outsourcing this work, which ensures faster turnaround and better quality control.

The company specializes in Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for both commercial and industrial properties, helping clients stay compliant with fire safety norms throughout the year without having to manage maintenance schedules themselves.

Gosafe's mission is to make Goa's homes, offices, factories, hotels, and public spaces safer through reliable fire detection, suppression, and prevention solutions, backed by a team of experienced and well-trained professionals.

Working hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM. Sundays by appointment for emergencies.

Gosafe serves clients across Goa including Madgaon, Panaji, Vasco, Ponda, Mapusa, Calangute, and surrounding industrial estates such as Verna, Cuncolim, and Sancoale.
