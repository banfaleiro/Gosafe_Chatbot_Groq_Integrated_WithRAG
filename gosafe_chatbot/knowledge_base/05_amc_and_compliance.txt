ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACTS (AMC) AND COMPLIANCE AT GOSAFE

Gosafe specializes in Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for commercial and industrial properties across Goa, including hotels, resorts, restaurants, factories, warehouses, hospitals, offices, malls, and residential societies.

WHAT AN AMC WITH GOSAFE INCLUDES
- Scheduled periodic inspection of all fire extinguishers, hose reels, hydrants, alarm panels, and suppression systems
- Refilling and servicing of extinguishers as per due dates
- Functional testing of fire alarm panels, detectors, and sounders
- Testing of fire pumps, jockey pumps, and hydrant systems
- Sprinkler system inspection and testing
- Replacement recommendations for expired or damaged equipment
- Detailed service reports and compliance documentation after every visit
- Priority response for breakdowns and emergency call-outs
- Assistance with fire department inspections and fire NOC renewals

WHY AMC MATTERS
Fire safety equipment must be regularly inspected, tested, and maintained to remain effective and to comply with local fire department regulations and insurance requirements. Many commercial and industrial fire NOC renewals require proof of a valid maintenance contract and up-to-date service records. An AMC with Gosafe ensures equipment is always audit-ready and that clients are not scrambling to fix compliance gaps before an inspection.

AMC PLANS
Gosafe offers flexible AMC plans that can be tailored to the size and risk profile of a property - ranging from small offices with a handful of extinguishers to large industrial sites with full suppression, hydrant, and alarm infrastructure. Visit frequency (quarterly, half-yearly, or annual) is customized based on the client's occupancy type and statutory requirements.
