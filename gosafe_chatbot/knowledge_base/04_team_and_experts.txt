GOSAFE TEAM AND EXPERTS

Gosafe's strength lies in its team of experienced fire safety professionals who bring together decades of hands-on field expertise, both in India and internationally.

LEAD EXPERT: MR. RUSSEL FALEIRO
Mr. Russel Faleiro is the lead fire safety expert at Gosafe, with over 33 years of experience in the fire safety and fire protection industry. His career includes 18 years of experience working in the Gulf region, where he was involved in large-scale fire protection projects across commercial, industrial, and high-rise developments. This international exposure has given him deep expertise in global fire safety standards, large-scale suppression system design, and best practices that he now applies to projects across Goa.

Mr. Faleiro personally oversees complex installations, fire suppression system design, and AMC service quality at Gosafe, ensuring that clients receive solutions that meet both Indian regulatory requirements and internationally recognized fire safety standards.

TEAM
Beyond Mr. Faleiro, Gosafe employs a team of trained technicians, certified electricians, and service engineers who specialize in fire alarm systems, extinguisher servicing, hydrant and sprinkler installation, and CCTV/security system setup. The team undergoes regular training to stay current with the latest fire safety codes, equipment, and technologies.

Gosafe's technicians are equipped to handle everything from a single fire extinguisher refill to the complete design and commissioning of a fire suppression system for a large industrial facility.
