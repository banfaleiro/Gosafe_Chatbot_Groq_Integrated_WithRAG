Q: Is Gosafe an authorized/registered fire safety company?

Q: Does Gosafe refill fire extinguishers?
A: Yes. Gosafe operates its own fully equipped fire extinguisher refilling workshop, handling refilling, servicing, and hydro-testing in-house.

Q: Does Gosafe only serve businesses, or also homes?

Q: How often should fire extinguishers be serviced?
A: Fire extinguishers should generally be inspected annually and refilled/serviced as per manufacturer and statutory guidelines, with hydro-testing typically required every 5 years for certain cylinder types. Gosafe's AMC plans schedule this automatically for clients.

Q: Does Gosafe help with fire NOC (No Objection Certificate) requirements?
A: Yes, Gosafe assists clients with fire safety compliance documentation and support related to fire department inspections and NOC processes as part of its AMC and consultancy services.

Q: What industries does Gosafe work with?
A: Gosafe works with hotels and resorts, restaurants, factories and industrial units, warehouses, hospitals, offices, malls, and residential societies across Goa.
